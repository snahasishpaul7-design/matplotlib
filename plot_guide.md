# Quick Chart Guide

- **Line plot:** Show trends or changes over time, such as daily sales.
- **Bar chart:** Compare values across categories, such as product sales.
- **Horizontal bar chart:** Compare categories when labels are long.
- **Pie chart:** Show how a small number of categories make up a whole.
- **Scatter plot:** Explore the relationship between two numeric variables.
- **Histogram:** Show how numeric values are distributed across ranges.
- **Subplots:** Place multiple charts together for comparison.
- **Area plot:** Highlight a trend with the area beneath the line filled in.
- **Stacked area plot:** Compare how several parts contribute to a changing total.
- **Box plot:** Compare spread, median, and possible outliers.
- **Error bar plot:** Show measurements with their uncertainty or variation.
- **Save a plot:** Use the savefig function to save a chart as an image file.
